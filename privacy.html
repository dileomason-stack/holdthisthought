Privacy Policy

Hold This Thought

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Hold This Thought is built so your thoughts stay yours. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The developer does not collect, see, sell, or share your data. Hold This Thought is made by Mason DiLeo, an independent developer.

Your thoughts

Your thoughts, reminders, photos you attach, saved places, and settings are stored on your device. If iCloud backup is on (Settings → Backup), a copy is also saved to your own private iCloud account so you can restore it on a new iPhone. That copy is in Apple's CloudKit private database: only you can access it, not the developer. You can turn backup off anytime. Photos are saved without the hidden location data cameras add.

Friends and shared thoughts

When you add a friend, you pick them from your contacts. Their name and phone number or email are used on your iPhone to address the invitation and to find their Apple Account through Apple's iCloud, so only they can open it. The invitation is sent as a text from your Messages app. The developer never receives your contacts.

When you share a thought with a friend, its words, time, and place (if it has one) are saved in a space in your iCloud that only you and that friend can open, and a copy appears in their app. They see when you check it off, and you see when they do. Your first name, as set in the app, is shown to them. Removing or blocking a friend ends the sharing; thoughts already shared stay on their phone as their own.

Groups (iCloud)

If you create or join a group, that group's tasks, member names, and optional places are stored in the group creator's iCloud using Apple's CloudKit and shared only with people they invite. The developer cannot access them. Leaving a group removes it from your iCloud; deleting a group you created removes it for everyone.

If a group task has a place, the first member to arrive claims it: the group sees who claimed it and when, so other members aren’t reminded too.

Location

Location is used only to deliver reminders you tie to a place, like "when I get home" or "next time I'm at Trader Joe's." Whether you've arrived is checked on your device. To find a place you haven't saved, the app searches Apple Maps, which receives the search and your approximate area under Apple's Privacy Policy. Your location is never sent to the developer. A place you attach to a shared thought is visible to the friend you share it with.

Microphone and speech

When you tap the mic, your words are turned into text using Apple's speech recognition. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process the audio under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app does not store recordings.

Calendar, camera, and photos

Calendar events are read on your device to time reminders around your schedule (for example, "before the party"), and events are only added when you ask. Photos you take or pick are stored on your device with the thought.

Apple Intelligence

On supported iPhones, short titles for your thoughts are written by Apple's on-device model. Your words don't leave the device for this.

Apple's role

The app relies on Apple's services (iCloud/CloudKit, Apple Maps, speech recognition, and notifications). Apple handles that data under its own Privacy Policy. No other companies are involved.

Deleting your data

Delete a thought by swiping it, or delete the app to remove everything on your iPhone. To delete the iCloud copy, turn off backup in the app, then open iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Hold This Thought → Delete Data. Because the developer never has your data, there is nothing for the developer to delete, but you can email with any request.

Do Not Track

The app doesn't track you across apps or websites, so there is nothing for a Do Not Track signal to turn off.

Children

Hold This Thought isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them. The developer doesn't collect personal information from anyone.

Your rights (including EU and UK)

You control your data directly on your device and in your iCloud, and can view, change, or delete it at any time as described above. If you have a question or request about your data, email the address below. If you’re in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and significant changes will be mentioned in the app's update notes.
