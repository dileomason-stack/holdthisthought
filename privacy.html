Privacy Policy

Hold This Thought

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Hold This Thought is built so your thoughts stay yours. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The developer does not collect, see, sell, or share your data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your thoughts, reminders, photos you attach, saved places, and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Groups and shared tasks (iCloud)

If you create or join a group, or share a task, that group's tasks, names, and optional place are stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit and shared only with the people you invite. The developer cannot access them. Leaving or deleting a group removes it from your iCloud.

Location

Location is used only to deliver reminders you tie to a place, like "when I get home." It is checked on your device and never sent to the developer or anyone else.

Microphone and speech

When you tap the mic, your words are turned into text using Apple's speech recognition. Depending on your device and settings, Apple may process the audio under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app does not store recordings.

Calendar, contacts, camera, and photos

Calendar events are read on your device to time reminders around your schedule, and events are only added when you ask. Contacts are used only when you choose someone to invite. Photos you take or pick are stored on your device with the thought.

Apple Intelligence

On supported iPhones, short titles for your thoughts are written by Apple's on-device model. Your words don't leave the device for this.

Children

The app doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
