Everything you need, nothing you have to set up.

Voice first

Capture a thought in seconds. Each one gets a short, clean title automatically.

📍

Places

Get reminded when you get home, get to work, or walk into the store.

Smart snooze

Long-press a reminder to snooze it, or tap “Next time I'm here” to get it on your next arrival.

Repeating

“Every morning,” “every Monday at 6,” “rent on the 1st.” Check it off and it comes back on schedule.

👥

Groups

Share tasks with roommates or family. Only the first person to get home gets the reminder.

🔒

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your thoughts stay on your iPhone, backed up to your own iCloud.
