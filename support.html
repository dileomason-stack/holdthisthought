Support

Hold This Thought

Need help or have an idea? Email holdthisthought.app@gmail.com and you'll hear back within a couple of days.

Reminders aren't showing up

Open iPhone Settings → Hold This Thought → Notifications and turn on Allow Notifications, Lock Screen, Sounds, and Badges.

"When I get home" reminders don't fire

Open iPhone Settings → Hold This Thought → Location and choose Always with Precise Location on. Then make sure Home is saved in the app's Places tab.

Snoozing

Long-press a reminder notification for Done and Snooze. Choose which snooze times appear in the app under Settings → Customize → Snooze.

Groups

Everyone in a group needs the app and to be signed in to iCloud. Only the person who created a group can invite or remove people.

Friends

To add a friend, go to the Friends tab, tap Add a friend, pick them from your contacts, and send the text. They tap the link to accept. Both of you need the app and to be signed in to iCloud. If the app asks for their Apple Account, ask them for the email or phone number they use for iCloud.

Report abuse or ask for something to be removed

From a friend's page or a thought someone shared with you, tap Report or Block. In a group, swipe left on a member. You can also email holdthisthought.app@gmail.com with who and what happened. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours, and removal requests, including for intimate images shared without consent, are handled within 48 hours.
